Your own villa, inside a larger community

Buying a home today means looking at more than the house. Location, nearby facilities, open space and lifestyle all matter. Vaarivana by Pharande Spaces brings villa living, green spaces and recreational facilities together in one large development.

Unlike a regular apartment project, Vaarivana is built around independent living. Residents get their own private space and still belong to a community with landscaped areas, recreation and places to spend time with family and neighbours.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Vaarivana by Pharande Spaces located?

Vaarivana is in Urse Central, Pune, near the Mumbai–Pune Expressway.

What is the total area of Vaarivana?

The project is spread across approximately 247 acres.

What types of homes are available?

The project overview mentions 3, 4 and 5 BHK private and twin villas, along with golf villa plots. The residential listing also highlights 4 BHK independent villas.

Does Vaarivana have a golf course?

Yes. It features a 6-hole golf course as part of its golf villa community.

What are the main amenities?

An 80,000 sq. ft. clubhouse, sports facilities, play parks, nature trails, a butterfly park, a natural stream, a pond, a waterfall and open garden cafés.

How is Vaarivana connected to Pune?

It sits along the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, with road connectivity towards Hinjawadi, Baner, Chakan, Talawade and other nearby destinations.
